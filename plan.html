The seven rules

  1. Paid on cash collected, never on signature. If the client doesn't pay the last instalment, nobody gets it.
  2. Everything is written before we start. A line in the log says who brought the lead, who closed, who delivers and with which weights. No line, no payment.
  3. One contract, one owner. They answer the client and trigger the payments. By default, the sector lead.
  4. Hats stack. Bring, close and deliver the same contract, and you get all three shares.
  5. You can sell the other sector. A marketing member who closes a Gold website gets the Closing share; Delivery goes to the Web sector.
  6. A mixed contract is split line by line. The website follows the one-off grid, the monthly pack the recurring grid.
  7. A dispute is settled by two. Youssef and Haroun decide together, and the decision is written down.

Every client payment and every payout is logged in the payments ledger. Payout within 7 days of the client paying.
