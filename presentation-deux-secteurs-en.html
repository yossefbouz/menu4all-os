The principle

You get paid for the work you did.
Not for being here.

Every dinar collected on a contract is cut into fixed shares. Each share goes to the person who did the matching work: finding, closing, building, having built the base. The rest goes to the shared fund.

No salary, no "founder" cut on every contract. The base (the reusable core code) is paid because it is reused.

The seven rules
  1. Paid on cash collected, never on signature. If the client doesn't pay the last instalment, nobody gets it.
  2. Everything is written before we start. Who brought the lead, who closed, who delivers, with which weights. No line, no payment.
  3. One contract, one owner. They answer the client and trigger the payments.
  4. Hats stack. Bring, close and deliver the same contract, and you get all three shares.
  5. You can sell the other sector. A marketing member who closes a Gold website gets the Closing share.
  6. A mixed contract is split line by line. The website follows the one-off grid, the monthly pack the recurring grid.
  7. A dispute is settled by two. Youssef and Haroun decide together, and it's written down.

Every client payment and every payout is logged in the payments ledger. Payout within 7 days.
