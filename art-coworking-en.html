    Why bundles?

    The customer pays once, up front, and stops worrying about time. For Art+, the drink is a guaranteed sale and the seat is paid for even if the customer leaves early.

    Why a student rate?

    Students fill the room during quiet hours. A visible discount brings them back, and word of mouth does the rest.
